Who We Are

Beauty rooted in African nature

RASAMI Beauty Natural Skincare was born from a deep love for Africa's extraordinary botanical heritage. We believe that the continent's richest ingredient — its nature — holds the key to radiant, healthy skin for every African woman and man.

Our formulas are crafted with purpose: no harmful chemicals, no empty promises. Every product is a carefully balanced blend of natural botanical actives, designed to work in harmony with melanin-rich skin.

Meet the Founder

The vision behind RASAMI Beauty

CEO & Founder

Founder & CEO

Amira Aliyu Abarshi

Beauty Expert · Formulator · Entrepreneur

Amira Aliyu Abarshi is a trained beauty expert and skincare formulator with a deep passion for harnessing the power of Africa's natural ingredients. With years of experience in cosmetic formulation and a relentless commitment to clean beauty, she founded RASAMI Beauty to fill a gap she experienced firsthand — the lack of premium, truly natural skincare products made specifically for African skin.

Her expertise spans product development, ingredient science, and skin health, with a particular focus on melanin-rich skin types. Amira has dedicated her career to studying how botanicals sourced from across Africa can be transformed into effective, luxurious skincare solutions that rival international brands — at honest, accessible prices.

Under her leadership, RASAMI Beauty has grown from a personal passion project into a brand trusted by skincare enthusiasts across Nigeria — with a mission to redefine what natural luxury looks like for African women and men.
